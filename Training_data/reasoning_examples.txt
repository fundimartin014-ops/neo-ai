Question: What happens if a computer has no power?
Reasoning: Computers require electricity to operate. Without power, components cannot function.
Answer: The computer will not turn on.

Question: Why is clean water important for cows?
Reasoning: Cows drink water daily. Dirty water can cause disease and reduce milk production.
Answer: Clean water keeps cows healthy and productive.

Question: Can offline AI work without internet?
Reasoning: Offline AI relies on local data. Internet is only needed for online search.
Answer: Yes, offline AI can work without internet.

Question: Why does more training data improve AI?
Reasoning: Training data provides examples. More examples improve understanding and accuracy.
Answer: More training data improves AI responses.

Question: What should AI do if it does not know an answer?
Reasoning: Guessing causes errors. Honesty maintains trust.
Answer: AI should say it does not know.
